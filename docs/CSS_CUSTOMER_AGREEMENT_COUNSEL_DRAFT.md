# CSS Customer Agreement — Counsel Review Draft

Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER USE

This document is a product/legal specification for counsel review. It is not a
jurisdictionally approved agreement and must not be activated in production
until the applicable legal-review record and production-commercialization
certification are both approved.

## 1. Parties and Service

The agreement must identify the CSS contracting entity, the customer, the
covered account(s), the service tier, the governing agreement ID/version, and
the effective date.

CSS must describe the service accurately as an advisory/decision-support
platform unless and until separately approved execution authority exists.
Nothing in this agreement may imply guaranteed profit, guaranteed recovery,
risk-free trading, or guaranteed availability.

## 2. Trial Period

The customer must be shown before acceptance:

- exact trial length;
- exact trial start timestamp;
- exact trial expiry timestamp;
- the paid pricing terms that begin after the trial;
- how and where to cancel;
- the cancellation cutoff; and
- a prominent automatic-conversion disclosure.

The product must derive the exact expiry from the governing agreement. The
customer must not supply or modify the expiry timestamp.

Required disclosure concept:

> Your free trial ends on [EXACT DATE/TIME]. If you do not cancel before that
> time, your paid CSS service will begin automatically under the pricing and
> terms shown here.

Final wording, prominence, notice timing, reminder obligations, and cancellation
mechanics must be approved by counsel for every offered jurisdiction.

## 3. Affirmative Acceptance

Enrollment requires separate affirmative confirmations that the customer:

1. reviewed and accepted the exact agreement version and pricing displayed; and
2. understood the trial duration and automatic conversion if not canceled
   before expiry.

CSS must retain immutable evidence of:

- customer/account identity;
- agreement ID and version;
- pricing-plan ID and displayed pricing;
- displayed conversion disclosure;
- acceptance timestamp;
- trial start and expiry;
- acceptance audit reference; and
- supporting evidence references.

Material pricing or charging changes require a new version and whatever
notice/re-acceptance process counsel determines is legally required.

## 4. CSS-Attributable Performance Compensation

The agreement must state the approved performance-compensation rate.

Performance compensation applies only to qualifying realized economic gain
attributable to accepted, compliant CSS advice.

The agreement must clearly explain:

- realized P&L only;
- CSS attribution criteria;
- the high-water-mark/loss-recovery mechanism;
- prior CSS-attributable losses are recovered before fresh gain becomes
  performance-fee eligible;
- gains used only to recover prior losses do not generate a CSS performance
  fee;
- CSS-attributable losses do not generate a performance fee; and
- the selected CSS performance fee cannot be replaced by a platform minimum.

Any examples must be illustrative and must not imply future performance.

## 5. Independent / Customer-Directed Activity

The agreement must distinguish:

- CSS advice accepted without material modification;
- customer-directed trading;
- materially modified CSS advice;
- outside/external trading; and
- any future mixed/partial attribution category approved by policy.

Independent/customer-directed economics must remain separate from CSS
performance attribution and must not alter CSS high-water-mark or loss-recovery
accounting.

Any platform/service-utilization charge for independent activity must state its
exact rate, charging base, frequency, cap/minimum if any, currency, and
calculation examples. Until those commercial terms receive final approval,
the current independent platform-charge implementation remains documentary /
shadow only.

External trades are excluded from CSS economics unless a later expressly
approved agreement says otherwise.

## 6. Fee Presentation and Billing

Customer statements must separately disclose:

- CSS-attributable realized P&L;
- loss recovered;
- fresh economic gain;
- CSS performance fee;
- independent/customer-directed realized P&L;
- independent platform/service charge, if applicable;
- gross customer result;
- total CSS charges; and
- customer net result after CSS charges.

Advice-level records should show:

advice -> customer P&L -> loss recovery -> fresh gain -> CSS fee -> customer
retained.

No fee may be collected solely because a documentary billable, invoice,
receivable, or payment-observation record exists.

## 7. Currency and FX

Cross-currency fee calculations must fail closed unless an approved FX evidence
record exists. The agreement must state the billing currency, permitted FX
source/method, relevant rate timing, and any FX spread or third-party fee.

## 8. Cancellation and Post-Cancellation Treatment

The agreement must specify:

- trial cancellation method;
- paid-service cancellation method;
- effective cancellation time;
- access after cancellation;
- treatment of already-earned charges;
- treatment of pending/uncrystallized economics;
- refunds or credits, if any; and
- data retention/export rules.

A cancellation timestamped before trial expiry must block automatic conversion.

## 9. Risk and Customer Responsibility

The final agreement must include counsel-approved disclosures that trading and
investing involve risk, losses can occur, past performance does not guarantee
future results, customers remain responsible for investment decisions except
to the extent a separately approved mandate expressly provides otherwise, and
CSS does not guarantee the accuracy or availability of third-party market,
broker, or data services.

## 10. Execution and Custody Boundaries

The agreement must accurately state whether CSS is advisory-only, confirm-only,
or operating under another separately approved execution model.

Commercial acceptance, fee eligibility, invoice status, or trial conversion
must never itself grant:

- broker execution authority;
- withdrawal authority;
- custody authority;
- client-funds deduction authority;
- automatic debit authority; or
- money-movement authority.

Those capabilities require independent regulatory, technical, contractual, and
production controls.

## 11. Privacy, Data and Security

The final agreement/privacy package must identify customer data categories,
purposes, retention, sharing/subprocessors, security expectations, customer
rights, cross-border treatment, and incident/contact procedures as required by
the applicable jurisdiction.

## 12. Complaints, Disputes and Governing Law

Counsel must complete the applicable complaint process, dispute-resolution
mechanism, venue/arbitration provisions if any, governing law, regulator or
ombudsman disclosures where required, and any mandatory non-waivable customer
rights.

## 13. Electronic Communications and Records

The agreement must address electronic delivery, notices, record retention,
version history, evidence of acceptance, and how customers can obtain a copy
of the agreement they accepted.

## 14. Amendments

Material changes to pricing, charging basis, trial conversion, customer rights,
or service scope must trigger the counsel-approved notice and re-acceptance
process. Silent replacement of an accepted material agreement version is not
permitted.

## 15. Preconditions to Production Charging

Production charging remains prohibited until all of the following are verified:

- jurisdiction-specific legal review approved;
- exact agreement version approved for that jurisdiction;
- customer accepted that same approved version;
- trial conversion status permits paid service, where applicable;
- no qualifying pre-expiry cancellation exists;
- pricing/fee calculation reconciles to canonical commercialization records;
- production commercialization certification is approved and unexpired;
- payment method/collection authority is separately approved;
- no security, reconciliation, or release-readiness blocker is active.

These checks are conjunctive. Any missing, stale, rejected, or contradictory
evidence must block charging.

## Counsel Completion Checklist

Counsel must explicitly approve or revise:

- contracting entity and capacity;
- jurisdiction eligibility;
- fee model and independent platform-charge basis;
- trial/automatic-renewal wording and notice requirements;
- cancellation/refund rules;
- advisory/execution regulatory characterization;
- required investment-risk disclosures;
- privacy/data terms;
- electronic communications/consent;
- complaints/dispute provisions;
- governing law and venue;
- tax/FX disclosures;
- mandatory cooling-off or withdrawal rights, if applicable;
- record-retention requirements; and
- any jurisdiction-specific consumer/investment protections.

Only the signed-off agreement version may be marked legally approved in the CSS
production charging gate.
