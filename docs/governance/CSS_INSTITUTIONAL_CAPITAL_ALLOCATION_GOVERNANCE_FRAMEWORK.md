CSS Institutional Capital Allocation Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the institutional capital allocation governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that capital deployment decisions remain disciplined, auditable, risk-adjusted, and aligned with portfolio objectives across all supported asset classes and broker environments.

---

Governance Principles

Capital allocation shall:

1. Preserve capital first.
2. Optimize risk-adjusted returns.
3. Prevent excessive concentration.
4. Support diversification.
5. Remain measurable.
6. Remain auditable.

---

Framework Objectives

The framework shall:

- Govern portfolio capital deployment.
- Govern asset-class allocations.
- Govern strategy allocations.
- Govern broker allocations.
- Govern risk-adjusted exposure.
- Support institutional portfolio management.

---

Capital Allocation Hierarchy

CSS shall allocate capital through the following hierarchy:

Level 1

Enterprise Capital

↓

Level 2

Broker Capital

↓

Level 3

Asset-Class Capital

↓

Level 4

Strategy Capital

↓

Level 5

Individual Position Capital

---

Asset-Class Allocation Governance

Capital allocations shall be governed for:

- Foreign Exchange
- Futures
- Options
- Equities
- ETFs
- Crypto Assets

Future asset classes shall participate through the same framework.

---

Allocation Categories

Strategic Allocation

Long-term allocation targets.

Characteristics:

- Stable
- Portfolio driven
- Reviewed periodically

---

Tactical Allocation

Short-term allocation adjustments.

Characteristics:

- Opportunity driven
- Risk adjusted
- Dynamic

---

Concentration Governance

The framework shall monitor:

- Asset concentration
- Sector concentration
- Strategy concentration
- Broker concentration
- Geographic concentration

---

Risk-Adjusted Allocation

Capital deployment decisions shall consider:

- Volatility
- Drawdown risk
- Liquidity
- Correlation
- Margin utilization
- Expected return

---

Reallocation Governance

Capital may be reallocated when:

- Risk conditions change
- Portfolio objectives change
- Asset-class attractiveness changes
- Broker risk changes

All reallocations shall be auditable.

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Capital allocations
- Allocation changes
- Concentration exposure
- Broker allocations
- Asset-class allocations
- Reallocation history

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative capital allocation governance standard for CSS.

Future portfolio engines, capital engines, broker integrations, and allocation models shall comply with this framework.
