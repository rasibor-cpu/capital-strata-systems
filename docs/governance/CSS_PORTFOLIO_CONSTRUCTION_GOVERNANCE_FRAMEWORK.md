CSS Portfolio Construction Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the portfolio construction governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that portfolio construction remains disciplined, diversified, risk-adjusted, auditable, and aligned with institutional investment standards.

---

Governance Principles

Portfolio construction shall:

1. Preserve capital.
2. Promote diversification.
3. Control concentration risk.
4. Maintain liquidity.
5. Remain risk-adjusted.
6. Remain auditable.

---

Framework Objectives

The framework shall:

- Govern portfolio construction.
- Govern portfolio diversification.
- Govern portfolio concentration.
- Govern cross-asset exposure.
- Govern risk-adjusted positioning.
- Support institutional portfolio management.

Diversification Governance

Portfolio construction shall consider:

- Asset-class diversification
- Strategy diversification
- Geographic diversification
- Currency diversification
- Sector diversification

---

Concentration Governance

Monitor:

- Single position concentration
- Asset-class concentration
- Strategy concentration
- Broker concentration

Excessive concentration shall require documented approval.

---

Correlation Governance

Portfolio construction shall evaluate:

- Asset correlations
- Strategy correlations
- Market regime correlations
- Stress-event correlations

Highly correlated positions shall be treated as elevated risk.

---

Liquidity Governance

Portfolio construction shall consider:

- Market liquidity
- Exit liquidity
- Stress liquidity
- Broker liquidity constraints

---

Risk Governance

Portfolio construction shall consider:

- Volatility
- Drawdown risk
- Margin utilization
- Leverage exposure
- Stress-event sensitivity

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Portfolio composition
- Diversification metrics
- Correlation metrics
- Concentration metrics
- Risk metrics
- Portfolio changes

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative portfolio construction governance standard for CSS.

Future portfolio engines, allocation engines, risk engines, and portfolio management modules shall comply with this framework.
