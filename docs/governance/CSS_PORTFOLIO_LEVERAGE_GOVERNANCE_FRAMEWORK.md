CSS Portfolio Leverage Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the portfolio leverage governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that leverage remains controlled, measurable, auditable, and aligned with institutional risk management standards across all supported asset classes.

---

Governance Principles

Portfolio leverage shall:

1. Support capital efficiency.
2. Preserve capital integrity.
3. Remain within approved limits.
4. Be continuously monitored.
5. Be fully auditable.
6. Never override risk controls.

---

Leverage Governance Objectives

The framework shall:

- Control portfolio leverage.
- Prevent excessive risk concentration.
- Support institutional capital management.
- Protect portfolio stability.
- Reduce margin-call risk.
- Support multi-asset expansion.

---

Leverage Categories

Conservative

Characteristics:

- Minimal leverage
- Strong capital reserves
- Low portfolio stress

Leverage Range:

1.0x – 1.5x

---

Moderate

Characteristics:

- Controlled leverage
- Diversified exposures
- Standard operating conditions

Leverage Range:

1.5x – 3.0x

---

Aggressive

Characteristics:

- Elevated leverage
- Increased portfolio sensitivity
- Enhanced monitoring required

Leverage Range:

3.0x – 5.0x

---

Critical

Characteristics:

- High leverage exposure
- Elevated liquidation risk
- Stress-event vulnerability

Leverage Range:

Greater than 5.0x

Immediate review required.

---

Asset-Class Leverage Governance

Foreign Exchange

Monitor:

- Pair concentration
- Currency exposure
- Aggregate leverage

---

Futures

Monitor:

- Contract leverage
- Expiration concentration
- Volatility exposure

---

Options

Monitor:

- Notional exposure
- Greeks exposure
- Assignment risk

---

Equities

Monitor:

- Margin utilization
- Sector concentration
- Liquidity profile

---

ETFs

Monitor:

- Leveraged ETF exposure
- Underlying concentration
- Portfolio correlation

---

Crypto Assets

Monitor:

- Volatility-adjusted leverage
- Concentration exposure
- Liquidity conditions

---

Portfolio Exposure Governance

The system shall evaluate:

- Gross exposure
- Net exposure
- Long exposure
- Short exposure
- Leverage-adjusted exposure
- Cross-asset exposure

---

Breach Management

Warning Threshold

Leverage approaches approved operating limits.

Required Action:

- Enhanced monitoring
- Risk review

---

Critical Threshold

Leverage exceeds approved limits.

Required Action:

- Immediate escalation
- Exposure reduction review
- Risk assessment

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Portfolio leverage
- Gross exposure
- Net exposure
- Asset-class leverage
- Concentration exposure
- Breach events

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative leverage governance standard for CSS.

Future margin engines, broker integrations, portfolio engines, and capital allocation modules shall comply with this framework.
