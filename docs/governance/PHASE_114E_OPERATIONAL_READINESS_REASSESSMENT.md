# Phase 114E: Operational Readiness Reassessment

## Objective
Consolidate the findings from Phase 112A (Final Production Certification Readiness), Phase 112B (Controlled Live Go/No-Go), Phase 113F (Controlled Micro-Live Re-Certification), and the newly established Phase 114 framework to formally state the current operational readiness posture of Capital Strata Systems.

## Review Context
1. **Phase 112A & 112B:** Verified the architectural separation of broker credentials and risk guardrails, establishing the initial Micro-Live checklist.
2. **Phase 113F:** Concluded with a status of "READY WITH CONDITIONS" for Micro-Live, purely contingent on Coinbase key rotation.
3. **Phase 114 Operational Validation:** Established the rigor required to transition from pure unit-test certification to sustained, multi-day operational endurance proof.

## Current Readiness Status Declarations

### 1. Controlled Paper Validation
**READY**
- The system is fundamentally secure, logically consistent, and architecturally unified. It is unconditionally cleared to commence the multi-day (48-72 hour) Phase 114D operational validation runs.

### 2. Controlled Micro-Live Execution
**READY WITH CONDITIONS**
- **Condition 1:** The operations team MUST rotate the Coinbase API credentials as determined in Phase 113E.
- **Condition 2:** The system MUST successfully clear the Phase 114D multi-day paper validation run (minimum 48 hours continuous stability) and present the Phase 114C evidence bundle before any real capital is bound.

### 3. Institutional Production Execution
**NOT READY**
- The system is architecturally capable, but Institutional scaling requires empirical, long-term statistical validation from the Micro-Live phase. It is fundamentally prohibited to deploy institutional size until Micro-Live operational parameters prove standard deviation safety over extensive market cycles.
