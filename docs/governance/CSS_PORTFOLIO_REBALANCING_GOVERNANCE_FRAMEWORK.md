CSS Portfolio Rebalancing Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the portfolio rebalancing governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that portfolio allocations remain aligned with approved risk targets, diversification objectives, capital allocation policies, and institutional investment standards.

---

Governance Principles

Portfolio rebalancing shall:

1. Preserve capital.
2. Maintain diversification.
3. Maintain allocation discipline.
4. Control concentration risk.
5. Remain risk-adjusted.
6. Remain auditable.

---

Framework Objectives

The framework shall:

- Maintain target allocations.
- Control allocation drift.
- Maintain diversification.
- Control portfolio concentration.
- Improve risk-adjusted portfolio performance.
- Support institutional portfolio management.

---

Rebalancing Categories

Scheduled Rebalancing

Characteristics:

- Periodic review
- Planned execution
- Allocation-driven

Examples:

- Monthly review
- Quarterly review
- Annual review

---

Threshold Rebalancing

Characteristics:

- Trigger-based
- Allocation drift driven

Examples:

- Asset-class exceeds target range
- Strategy exceeds target range
- Broker allocation exceeds target range

---

Risk Rebalancing

Characteristics:

- Risk-event driven
- Portfolio protection focused

Examples:

- Volatility spike
- Margin stress
- Concentration increase
- Correlation increase

---

Rebalancing Assessment Factors

Rebalancing decisions shall consider:

- Allocation drift
- Portfolio volatility
- Correlation changes
- Liquidity conditions
- Margin utilization
- Capital efficiency
- Market conditions

---

Allocation Drift Governance

The framework shall monitor:

- Asset-class drift
- Strategy drift
- Broker drift
- Portfolio drift

Significant drift shall trigger review.

---

Risk Governance

Rebalancing decisions shall consider:

- Drawdown risk
- Concentration risk
- Correlation risk
- Liquidity risk
- Margin risk
- Leverage risk

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Rebalancing events
- Allocation drift
- Rebalancing decisions
- Portfolio adjustments
- Risk impacts
- Historical rebalancing activity

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative portfolio rebalancing governance standard for CSS.

Future portfolio engines, capital allocation engines, and portfolio management modules shall comply with this framework.
