CSS Strategy Capital Allocation Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the strategy capital allocation framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to govern how portfolio capital is allocated among trading strategies while maintaining diversification, capital preservation, risk control, and institutional portfolio management standards.

---

Governance Principles

Strategy allocation shall:

1. Preserve capital.
2. Reward demonstrated performance.
3. Control concentration risk.
4. Remain risk-adjusted.
5. Remain measurable.
6. Remain auditable.

---

Framework Objectives

The framework shall:

- Allocate capital among strategies.
- Control strategy concentration.
- Improve diversification.
- Improve capital efficiency.
- Improve portfolio resilience.
- Support institutional portfolio management.

---

Strategy Categories

The framework shall support allocation across:

- Trend Following
- Mean Reversion
- Momentum
- Volatility
- Market Neutral
- Arbitrage
- Income Generation
- Future Approved Strategies

---

Allocation Assessment Factors

Strategy allocations shall consider:

- Historical performance
- Drawdown characteristics
- Volatility
- Risk-adjusted return
- Correlation with other strategies
- Capital efficiency
- Liquidity requirements

---

Strategy Concentration Governance

Monitor:

- Capital allocated to each strategy
- Strategy contribution to portfolio risk
- Strategy contribution to portfolio return
- Cross-strategy correlations

No strategy shall dominate portfolio capital without documented approval.

---

Allocation Adjustments

Capital allocations may be increased when:

- Risk-adjusted performance improves
- Portfolio diversification benefits improve
- Capital efficiency improves

Capital allocations may be reduced when:

- Drawdowns increase
- Risk increases
- Diversification benefits decline
- Performance deteriorates

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Strategy allocations
- Allocation changes
- Strategy performance
- Strategy risk metrics
- Capital utilization
- Reallocation history

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative strategy capital allocation standard for CSS.

Future portfolio engines, allocation engines, strategy engines, and governance modules shall comply with this framework.
