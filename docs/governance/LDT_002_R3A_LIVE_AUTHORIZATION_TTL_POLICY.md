# LDT-002 R3A Live Authorization TTL Policy

Status: OWNER APPROVED FOR CONTROLLED IMPLEMENTATION

Scope: Final pre-submit controlled live-order authorization freshness only.

## Canonical Rules

1. TTL is fixed at 60 seconds.
2. Time is UTC only, timezone-aware only, ISO-8601 UTC only.
3. issued_at is generated by the final authorization component.
4. expires_at = issued_at + 60 seconds.
5. Valid interval is issued_at <= evaluation_time < expires_at.
6. No positive clock-skew allowance; future-issued authorization is invalid.
7. Authorization is single-use after a successful consume attempt and must not be reused.
8. Authorization scope is bound at minimum to:
   - authorization_id
   - order identity
   - environment
   - broker
   - account
   - symbol
   - side
   - authoritative exposure amount
   - authoritative exposure currency
9. LIVE authorization can authorize LIVE only.
10. Revocation invalidates authorization immediately.
11. Kill switch has absolute precedence.
12. Clearing kill switch does not resurrect an old authorization.
13. Final authorization does not survive process restart.
14. No automatic renewal is allowed.
15. Final freshness decision is owned by the final pre-submit boundary.

## Deterministic Evidence Requirements

Each decision must expose at minimum:

- authorization_id
- issued_at
- expires_at
- evaluation_time
- ttl_seconds
- scope_fingerprint
- freshness_result
- consumed
- revoked
- kill_switch_active
- decision
- rejection_reason

## Stable Rejection Reasons

- missing_authorization
- malformed_authorization
- missing_issued_at
- malformed_issued_at
- future_issued_at
- missing_expires_at
- invalid_expiry
- authorization_expired
- authorization_revoked
- authorization_already_consumed
- authorization_scope_mismatch
- environment_mismatch
- broker_mismatch
- account_mismatch
- instrument_mismatch
- order_mismatch
- kill_switch_active

## Authority Boundaries

This policy does not enable live trading and does not bypass:

- RBAC
- live toggle
- two-key arming
- readiness
- kill switch
- execution gate
- AntiBleed
- Phase 152A CAD 20 governor
- R2A CAD-only currency authority
- broker certification

Trading authority must not increase.
