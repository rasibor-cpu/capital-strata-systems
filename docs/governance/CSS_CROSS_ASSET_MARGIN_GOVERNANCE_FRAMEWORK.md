CSS Cross-Asset Margin Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the cross-asset margin governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that margin utilization, leverage exposure, and capital allocation are evaluated across the entire portfolio rather than at individual asset-class level only.

---

Governance Principles

Cross-asset governance shall:

1. Treat portfolio risk holistically.
2. Prevent hidden leverage accumulation.
3. Control aggregate margin exposure.
4. Protect capital during stress events.
5. Support institutional portfolio management.
6. Remain auditable and measurable.

---

Framework Objectives

The framework shall:

- Aggregate margin exposure.
- Measure portfolio-wide leverage.
- Identify concentration risk.
- Identify correlation risk.
- Improve capital allocation.
- Support institutional risk controls.

---

Covered Asset Classes

The framework applies to:

- Foreign Exchange
- Futures
- Options
- Equities
- ETFs
- Crypto Assets

Future asset classes shall automatically participate in cross-asset margin calculations.

---

Portfolio Margin Components

CSS shall evaluate:

Asset-Class Margin Usage

Measure:

- FX margin
- Futures margin
- Options margin
- Equity margin
- ETF margin
- Crypto margin

---

Aggregate Margin Usage

Measure:

- Total margin utilized
- Total available margin
- Free margin
- Margin utilization ratio

---

Portfolio Leverage

Measure:

- Gross leverage
- Net leverage
- Effective leverage
- Stress-adjusted leverage

---

Correlation Governance

The system shall identify:

- Highly correlated positions
- Sector concentration
- Geographic concentration
- Macro concentration
- Currency concentration

Correlated positions may increase effective portfolio risk even when individual positions appear compliant.

---

Concentration Governance

Monitor:

- Single asset concentration
- Single strategy concentration
- Single broker concentration
- Single asset-class concentration

---

Stress Governance

The framework shall support evaluation of:

- Market stress events
- Liquidity stress events
- Volatility expansion events
- Broker disruptions
- Margin expansion scenarios

---

Breach Classification

Warning

Portfolio metrics approach approved limits.

Required Action:

- Enhanced monitoring
- Risk review

---

Critical

Portfolio metrics exceed approved limits.

Required Action:

- Immediate escalation
- Exposure review
- Capital preservation actions

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Cross-asset margin utilization
- Aggregate leverage
- Correlation exposure
- Concentration exposure
- Stress exposure
- Breach history

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative cross-asset margin governance standard for CSS.

Future margin engines, portfolio engines, broker integrations, and capital allocation modules shall comply with this framework.
