CSS Asset Class Capital Allocation Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the asset class capital allocation framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to define how capital is allocated, monitored, adjusted, and governed across supported asset classes while maintaining diversification, capital preservation, and institutional risk standards.

---

Governance Principles

Asset class allocation shall:

1. Preserve capital.
2. Support diversification.
3. Avoid excessive concentration.
4. Remain risk-adjusted.
5. Remain measurable.
6. Remain auditable.

---

Supported Asset Classes

The framework applies to:

- Foreign Exchange (FX)
- Futures
- Options
- Equities
- ETFs
- Crypto Assets

Future asset classes shall participate through the same governance framework.

---

Allocation Objectives

The framework shall:

- Govern asset-class exposure.
- Improve diversification.
- Reduce concentration risk.
- Improve capital efficiency.
- Support portfolio stability.
- Support institutional portfolio management.

---

Strategic Allocation Model

Strategic allocation represents long-term target allocations.

Characteristics:

- Portfolio driven
- Risk adjusted
- Reviewed periodically
- Stable over time

---

Tactical Allocation Model

Tactical allocation represents temporary adjustments.

Characteristics:

- Opportunity driven
- Dynamic
- Market responsive
- Risk controlled

---

Allocation Assessment Factors

Capital allocation decisions shall consider:

- Volatility
- Liquidity
- Drawdown risk
- Correlation
- Margin requirements
- Expected return
- Market conditions

---

Concentration Governance

Monitor:

- Asset-class concentration
- Cross-asset concentration
- Strategy concentration
- Broker concentration

No single asset class shall dominate portfolio exposure without documented approval.

---

Reallocation Triggers

Capital allocations may be adjusted when:

- Market conditions change
- Risk conditions change
- Portfolio objectives change
- Liquidity conditions change
- Broker risk changes

All reallocations shall be recorded and auditable.

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Asset-class allocations
- Allocation changes
- Concentration exposure
- Allocation performance
- Reallocation history

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative asset class allocation standard for CSS.

Future portfolio engines, capital allocation engines, broker integrations, and portfolio governance modules shall comply with this framework.
