# Phase 180 — Enterprise Governance and Certification Readiness

The Enterprise Governance subsystem is a read-only evidence projection across
security, identity, OAuth, secrets, broker runtime, Options Income, trading
runtime, reporting, audit, compliance, operations, risk, and business
continuity.

ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 results are readiness percentages only. CSS does not
claim ISO certification. A control is satisfied only by supplied, verified
evidence with a traceable reference. Missing and failed evidence remain
explicit blockers.

Business-continuity readiness covers backup, restore, disaster recovery,
recovery objectives, redundancy, and infrastructure resilience. No backup,
restore, or recovery operation is initiated by governance evaluation.

The enterprise risk register is immutable after construction and covers
operational, technical, security, provider, broker, market-data, and regulatory
risk categories.

The certification-readiness engine evaluates only supplied evidence. It never
creates substitute evidence or infers production certification.

Safety posture remains:

- execution `DISABLED`
- authority `BLOCKED`
- runtime `FAIL_CLOSED`
- usage `ADVISORY_ONLY`

Formal production or ISO certification requires independent verification and
is outside Phase 180.
