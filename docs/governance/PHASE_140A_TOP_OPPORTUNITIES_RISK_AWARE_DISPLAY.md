# Phase 140A Top Opportunities Risk-Aware Display

Phase 140A makes top-opportunity display risk-aware and display-only.

- GREEN and APPROVED opportunities are shown first.
- If no GREEN or APPROVED opportunities exist, AMBER, WATCH, MONITOR_ONLY, and NEAR_APPROVED opportunities are shown.
- RED, REJECTED, BLOCKED, DENIED, and NOT_APPROVED opportunities are excluded.
- If every opportunity is blocked/red, the UI shows a capital-preservation empty state.
- Market Health and Opportunity Explanation fields are surfaced for operator context.

This phase does not place orders, change broker state, change RBAC, or alter trade gates.
