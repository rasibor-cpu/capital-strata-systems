CSS Institutional Margin Governance Framework

Purpose

This document establishes the institutional margin governance framework for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to ensure that leverage, margin utilization, capital efficiency, and portfolio exposure remain governed by formal institutional controls across all supported asset classes.

---

Governance Principles

CSS shall operate under the following margin principles:

1. Capital preservation first.
2. Margin utilization must be controlled.
3. Excessive leverage is prohibited.
4. Margin risk must be measurable.
5. Margin exposure must be auditable.
6. Margin controls shall operate consistently across asset classes.

---

Margin Governance Objectives

The framework shall:

- Protect capital.
- Limit excessive leverage.
- Prevent margin concentration.
- Support institutional risk management.
- Maintain operational discipline.
- Provide auditability.

---

Supported Asset Classes

This framework applies to:

- Foreign Exchange (FX)
- Futures
- Options
- Equities
- ETFs
- Crypto Assets

Future asset classes shall automatically inherit these governance principles.

---

Margin Risk Categories

Low Risk

Examples:

- Cash positions
- Fully funded positions
- Hedged exposures

Expected Margin Usage:

0% – 25%

---

Moderate Risk

Examples:

- Standard margin positions
- Limited leverage positions

Expected Margin Usage:

25% – 50%

---

Elevated Risk

Examples:

- Leveraged directional positions
- Multi-position exposure

Expected Margin Usage:

50% – 75%

---

Critical Risk

Examples:

- Excessive leverage
- Concentrated exposure
- Margin stress conditions

Expected Margin Usage:

75% – 100%

Immediate review required.

---

Margin Utilization Controls

CSS shall monitor:

- Used margin
- Available margin
- Margin ratio
- Free margin
- Portfolio leverage
- Asset-class leverage

Monitoring Frequency:

Continuous during active operations.

---

Margin Breach Governance

Warning Threshold

Margin utilization exceeds approved operating range.

Required Action:

- Operator review
- Exposure assessment

---

Critical Threshold

Margin utilization approaches predefined maximum limits.

Required Action:

- Immediate investigation
- Exposure reduction review
- Risk escalation

---

Cross-Asset Exposure Governance

CSS shall evaluate:

- FX exposure
- Futures exposure
- Options exposure
- Equity exposure
- ETF exposure
- Crypto exposure

Aggregate exposure shall remain consistent with approved portfolio risk limits.

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Margin utilization
- Free margin
- Leverage ratios
- Margin breaches
- Margin warnings
- Exposure summaries

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative institutional margin governance policy for CSS.

Future margin engines, broker integrations, and leverage controls shall comply with the requirements defined in this document.
