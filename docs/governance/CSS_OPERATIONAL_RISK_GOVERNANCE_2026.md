# Capital Strata Systems (CSS)
## Operational Risk Governance 2026

Status: Institutional Operational Risk Governance Framework

---

## Operational Risk Philosophy

CSS operational governance must prioritize:

- institutional safety
- controlled degradation
- reconciliation integrity
- governance continuity
- replay visibility
- audit traceability
- rollback readiness
- operational explainability

Operational continuity must NEVER override institutional safety controls.

---

## Core Operational Risk Areas

Primary operational risk domains:

- broker disconnects
- reconciliation drift
- websocket instability
- stale payload propagation
- governance visibility failure
- replay inconsistency
- execution sequencing failure
- permission inconsistency
- session integrity failure
- audit visibility degradation

## Controlled Degradation Philosophy

CSS must support controlled degradation behavior.

Examples:
- broker disconnect → execution restriction
- reconciliation drift → live-trading restriction
- websocket instability → synchronization warning state
- replay inconsistency → replay suspension
- governance visibility failure → operational escalation

Silent operational degradation is NOT acceptable.
