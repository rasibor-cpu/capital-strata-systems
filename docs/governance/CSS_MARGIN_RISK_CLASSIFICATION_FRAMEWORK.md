CSS Margin Risk Classification Framework

Purpose

This document defines the official margin risk classification methodology for Capital Strata Systems (CSS).

The objective is to establish a consistent framework for measuring, categorizing, monitoring, and governing margin-related risk across all supported asset classes.

---

Classification Principles

Margin risk classifications shall:

1. Be objective.
2. Be measurable.
3. Be auditable.
4. Be consistent.
5. Support institutional governance.

---

Risk Categories

Tier 1 – Conservative

Characteristics:

- Fully funded positions
- Minimal leverage
- Low volatility assets
- Hedged positions

Examples:

- Cash equity holdings
- Treasury ETFs
- Fully funded long positions

Risk Rating:

LOW

---

Tier 2 – Moderate

Characteristics:

- Standard margin utilization
- Moderate leverage
- Diversified exposure

Examples:

- Standard FX positions
- Diversified equity portfolios
- Broad-market ETF exposure

Risk Rating:

MODERATE

---

Tier 3 – Elevated

Characteristics:

- Significant leverage
- Increased volatility
- Concentrated positions

Examples:

- Leveraged ETF exposure
- Concentrated futures positions
- Aggressive directional trades

Risk Rating:

HIGH

---

Tier 4 – Critical

Characteristics:

- Excessive leverage
- Extreme concentration
- Stress-event vulnerability

Examples:

- Margin utilization exceeding approved limits
- Single-position concentration risk
- Portfolio stress scenarios

Risk Rating:

CRITICAL

Immediate review required.

---

Risk Assessment Factors

CSS shall evaluate:

- Margin utilization
- Portfolio leverage
- Asset volatility
- Concentration levels
- Liquidity characteristics
- Correlation exposure
- Stress-event sensitivity

---

Asset-Class Risk Considerations

FX

Monitor:

- Currency concentration
- Leverage levels
- Macroeconomic exposure

---

Futures

Monitor:

- Contract leverage
- Expiration exposure
- Volatility risk

---

Options

Monitor:

- Greeks exposure
- Assignment risk
- Volatility exposure

---

Equities

Monitor:

- Sector concentration
- Liquidity risk
- Market-cap exposure

---

ETFs

Monitor:

- Leverage characteristics
- Underlying concentration
- Liquidity profile

---

Crypto Assets

Monitor:

- Volatility exposure
- Liquidity conditions
- Concentration risk

---

Governance Actions

Low Risk

Normal monitoring.

Moderate Risk

Enhanced monitoring.

High Risk

Management review required.

Critical Risk

Immediate risk escalation required.

---

Reporting Requirements

The system shall support reporting of:

- Risk classifications
- Margin utilization
- Concentration exposure
- Leverage ratios
- Margin breaches
- Escalation events

---

Governance

This framework serves as the authoritative margin risk classification standard for CSS.

Future margin engines, portfolio governance modules, and broker integrations shall comply with this framework.
