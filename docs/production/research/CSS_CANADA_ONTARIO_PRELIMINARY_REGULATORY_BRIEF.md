# CSS Canada/Ontario Preliminary Regulatory Research Brief

Status: PRELIMINARY RESEARCH FOR COUNSEL — NOT A LEGAL OPINION
Research date: 2026-09-17

This brief reduces counsel discovery time if Canada/Ontario is included in the
launch scope. It does not establish that Canada/Ontario is the final launch
jurisdiction and does not authorize any CSS service mode.

## 1. Online / automated investment advice

Current CIRO materials show that online/hybrid/automated advice remains an
active regulatory policy area. CIRO's March 12, 2026 order-execution-only
guidance emphasizes that OEO dealers remain subject to the prohibition on
recommendations. CIRO is separately reviewing online and hybrid advice models.

Counsel should therefore determine, for each CSS mode:

- DISCOVER: whether outputs are sufficiently general/non-tailored to avoid
  constituting a recommendation;
- CONFIRM: whether customer-specific advice/recommendation triggers registration
  or dealer/adviser obligations;
- AUTO: whether automated implementation requires a separately regulated
  adviser/dealer/execution arrangement and what supervision/suitability/KYC
  obligations apply.

Official references:
- https://www.ciro.ca/newsroom/publications/guidance-order-execution-only-account-services-and-activities
- https://www.ciro.ca/rules-and-enforcement/dealer-member-rules/access-advice
- https://www.ciro.ca/newsroom/publications/access-online-advice-advisory-and-managed-channels

## 2. Ontario internet consumer agreements

Ontario's Consumer Protection Act framework requires internet-agreement
disclosure before contracting, an express opportunity to accept or decline and
correct errors, and delivery of a written copy of the agreement. Ontario
guidance also emphasizes written contract detail for subscription arrangements
and consumer cancellation rights where statutory requirements are not met.

CSS already contains:
- exact versioned agreement acceptance;
- affirmative accept controls;
- displayed pricing/trial disclosures;
- acceptance/cancellation evidence;
- contract copy/version tracking.

Counsel should confirm the current statutory/regulatory requirements applicable
to the final CSS agreement and any trial/automatic conversion design.

Official references:
- https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/02c30
- https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract
- https://www.ontario.ca/page/contracts-best-practices-and-types

## 3. Commercial electronic messages / notices

CRTC CASL guidance states that commercial electronic messages generally require
consent, identification/contact information and a functioning unsubscribe
mechanism. Unsubscribe requests must be acted on without delay and no later
than 10 business days. The sender bears the burden of proving consent.

CSS notice-policy approval should therefore distinguish:
- legally/contractually required service notices;
- transactional messages;
- commercial/marketing messages;
- consent evidence;
- unsubscribe applicability and processing;
- retention of consent/unsubscribe evidence.

Official references:
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/internet/anti/reg.htm

## 4. Privacy / third-party processors

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada states that PIPEDA requires
meaningful consent where applicable, appropriate safeguards based on
sensitivity, accountability, and protection of personal information handled by
third-party processors. Current OPC guidance also emphasizes assessing
third-party service providers and using contractual or other means to ensure
comparable protection.

Provider reviews should therefore cover:
- data categories transferred to payment/notification/cloud providers;
- purpose limitation and consent;
- retention/deletion;
- encryption and access control;
- sub-processors and cross-border processing;
- breach detection/notification;
- provider contractual safeguards.

Official references:
- https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/
- https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda-compliance-help/guide_org/
- https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/gd_third-party_202609/

## Counsel decision output required

Counsel should return an evidence record that states:

- jurisdiction;
- agreement ID/version;
- legal entity/supplier;
- customer type(s);
- DISCOVER determination;
- CONFIRM determination;
- AUTO determination;
- registration/licensing implications;
- permitted fee model;
- trial/automatic-conversion requirements;
- cancellation/refund requirements;
- payment-collection requirements;
- notice/CASL treatment;
- privacy/processor requirements;
- mandatory contract/disclosure changes;
- reviewer identity/reference;
- approval timestamp;
- immutable opinion/approval reference.

The output must map to the CSS production evidence package and must not be
represented as approved until counsel actually issues it.
