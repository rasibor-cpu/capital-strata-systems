\# Futures Architecture Baseline

Capital Strata Systems



Status: Architecture Locked  

Phase: 2A Pending Implementation  



---



\## Capital Structure



\- FX Capital Bucket: 75%

\- Futures Capital Bucket: 25%



Buckets are logically separated.

Governance Core is shared.



---



\## Governance Model



Futures must obey:



\- RiskGovernor caps

\- RegimeGate

\- Liquidity gate

\- Correlation gate

\- Execution firewall



No exceptions.



---



\## Activation Constraint



Futures trading remains disabled until:



\- 4 consecutive profitable FX weeks

\- No defensive regime trigger



Activation must be:



\- Logged

\- Policy-validated

\- Session-scoped



---



\## Rollout Phases



Phase 2A:

\- ES (S\&P 500)

\- NQ (Nasdaq)



Phase 2B:

\- CL (Crude Oil)



Phase 2C:

\- GC (Gold)

\- ZN (10Y Treasury)



---



\## Risk Constraints



Futures must operate under:



\- Stricter drawdown caps than FX

\- Tighter margin constraints

\- Independent position tracking

\- Shared global risk stop



---



Architecture remains governance-first.

Execution follows structure.
